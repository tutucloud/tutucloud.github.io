Privacy Policy — 24 Game

Effective date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the 24 Game application (the "App") handles information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary

The App does not collect, transmit, store, or share any user information. It requires no network access, has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. All data it produces stays on your device, under your control, and can be erased at any time by clearing the App's data or your browser data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect or process any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, or similar);
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or fingerprints;
  • Location data of any kind;
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports;
  • Cookies used for tracking, advertising, or profiling.

2. No Network Access

The App is a fully self-contained, offline application. It does not require, request, or establish any network connection. It does not communicate with any server, third-party service, or API. All computation — game logic, rendering, and scoring — is performed entirely on your device.

3. Local Data Storage

The App may store small amounts of non-personal data locally on your device (for example, game preferences or your best results). This data:

  • never leaves your device and is never transmitted to anyone;
  • is not accessible to us — we have no way to read it;
  • can be deleted at any time by clearing the App's data in your device or browser settings.

4. Third Parties

The App does not embed any third-party SDKs, analytics tools, advertisements, or tracking components, and does not share information with any third party.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences, including children. Because no information is collected from any user, no information is collected from children under any applicable age threshold.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, please reach out to the distributor through whom you obtained the App, or visit our feedback group.
