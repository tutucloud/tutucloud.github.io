This Privacy Policy describes how Tu-X ("we", "us") handles information in connection with the website tu-x.com and its services, software, tools and AI tools (collectively, the "Services").

Summary

Browser-based tools never send your data anywhere: everything runs on your device. AI tools are the only exception: their input is processed in the cloud by models officially deployed on Cloudflare. We do not run accounts, analytics or advertising systems of our own.

1. Browser-Based Tools and Applications

The tools in the "Tools" section and the listed web applications run entirely in your browser. Data you enter (documents, text, images, API keys for your own services) is processed locally and never transmitted to us or to any third party. Data saved between visits (e.g., localStorage, IndexedDB) stays on your device and can be cleared by you at any time.

2. AI Tools

AI tools send the input you submit to Cloudflare Workers AI, where it is processed by models officially deployed by Cloudflare. Your input does not leave Cloudflare's infrastructure, and per Cloudflare's model serving terms it is not used to train models. We do not log, store, or otherwise retain your AI inputs or outputs. AI usage is metered in aggregate (per plan quota) but not linked to your identity — the Services do not require an account.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect or process: personal identifiers (name, email, phone number); account credentials (the Services require no account); device identifiers or fingerprints; location data; usage analytics or telemetry; advertising cookies or trackers.

4. Infrastructure Processing

The Services are hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web property, standard infrastructure logs (e.g., IP addresses, request metadata) are processed by Cloudflare as the hosting provider under its own terms. We do not access or use such logs for profiling or tracking. Payments, when applicable, are arranged by email and processed by third-party payment providers; we only see the information you choose to include in those communications.

5. Cookies

The Services use only functional browser storage needed for features (language preference, theme, your local data). No tracking, advertising, or third-party analytics cookies are set.

6. Children's Privacy

The Services are suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under any applicable age threshold.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

8. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be sent to feedbacktu-xcom@googlegroups.com.
