This Refund Policy applies to paid subscription plans (Standard, Pro, VIP) for the Tu-X Services. The Free Plan involves no payment and no refunds.

1. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with a paid plan, you may request a full refund within 14 days of your initial payment for that billing period, for any reason, no questions asked.

2. Refunds After 14 Days

After the 14-day window, refunds may be granted on a case-by-case basis at our discretion, for example when:

  • a paid feature was unavailable for an extended period (e.g., AI resources continuously unavailable for more than 72 hours) and we could not resolve the issue;
  • you were charged incorrectly or twice for the same period;
  • we terminate the Services or materially downgrade a paid feature.

In such cases we may refund the current billing period in full or issue a pro-rata refund for the unused portion.

3. Non-Refundable Situations

  • Requests made more than 14 days after payment without the circumstances in Section 2;
  • Partial billing periods after voluntary cancellation (the plan remains active until the period ends);
  • Quota exhaustion caused by the subscriber's own usage, where the Services themselves performed as described;
  • Accounts terminated for violation of our Terms of Service.

4. How to Request a Refund

Send your request to feedbacktu-xcom@googlegroups.com with the subject line "Refund" and include the email address used for the subscription and the payment date. We respond within 5 business days. Approved refunds are returned via the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days depending on the payment provider.

5. Cancellation

You may cancel a subscription at any time by email. Cancellation stops future billing; the paid period remains active until its end. Cancelling does not by itself trigger a refund unless the conditions in Sections 1–2 are met.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The version in effect at the time of your payment governs that payment.

7. Contact

Questions about refunds may be sent to feedbacktu-xcom@googlegroups.com.
