Privacy Policy — 24 Game

Effective date: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the 24 Game web application (the "App"), available at https://tu-x.com/24game/ and operated by Tu-X ("we", "us"), handles information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Summary

We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information. The App has no network access requirements, no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising. All data it produces stays on your device, under your control, and can be erased at any time by clearing your browser data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not collect or process any of the following:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email address, phone number, or similar);
  • Device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or fingerprints;
  • Location data of any kind;
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports;
  • Cookies used for tracking, advertising, or profiling.

2. No Network Access

The App is a fully self-contained, offline-capable application. After the initial page load, it does not require, request, or establish any network connection. It does not communicate with any server, third-party service, or API. All computation — game logic, rendering, and scoring — is performed entirely within your browser on your own device.

3. Local Data Storage

The App may store small amounts of non-personal data on your device using standard browser mechanisms (such as localStorage), for example game preferences or your best results. This data:

  • never leaves your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party;
  • is not accessible to us — we have no way to read it;
  • can be deleted at any time by clearing your browser's site data for tu-x.com.

4. Third Parties

The App does not embed third-party SDKs, analytics tools, ads, or tracking scripts. The site is served as static files via GitHub Pages, which is subject to GitHub's own privacy statement for standard web hosting (e.g., server logs processed by GitHub as infrastructure provider). We do not have access to, and do not use, any such infrastructure-level data.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for general audiences, including children. Because no information is collected from any user, no information is collected from children under any applicable age threshold.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or the App, please contact us via the repository at github.com/tutucloud.
